FAQ

Chauffeur Service Questions

How is chauffeur service priced?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, the number of hours, and the route. Send a request or call us and we'll give you an exact quote.

Is gratuity included?

We'll spell out everything that's included when we send your quote, so there are no surprises on the day.

Do you track flights for airport pickups?

Yes. Give us your flight number and your chauffeur will adjust to delays and meet you on arrival.

Can the chauffeur wait between stops?

Yes. With hourly or full-day service your chauffeur stays with you for as many stops as you need.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and big events, book as early as you can. For everything else, same-day requests are often possible, so just call.
