FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before reserving your luxury or exotic rental.

What's included in the rental rate?

Every rental includes 125 miles per day as a standard allowance. Need more for your trip? Let us know when you book and we'll work out a mileage plan that fits.

Do I need my own insurance?

Yes — a valid personal auto insurance policy with adequate liability coverage is required for every exotic and luxury rental. Our team confirms your coverage meets our minimum requirements before delivery.

Is a security deposit required?

A refundable security deposit is required on every reservation. The exact amount depends on which vehicle you choose and is confirmed when you book.

Can you deliver the car to me?

Absolutely. We offer white-glove delivery and pickup anywhere in the region — to your home, hotel, venue, or the airport. Delivery details are arranged once you submit a reservation request.

Is a chauffeur available?

Yes. Chauffeur service is available as an add-on for select vehicles — a great option for weddings, events, or nights out where you'd rather not drive.

What's the minimum age and license requirement?

Renters must be at least 25 years old with a valid driver's license in good standing. Some vehicles may carry additional requirements, which we'll confirm at booking.
