Service Areas

We Bring the Car to You

Echelon Exotics is based in Massachusetts and delivers across the state and into Rhode Island, to homes, hotels, venues, and airports.

Complimentary Delivery

The first 10 miles of delivery and pickup are on us. Beyond that, delivery is quoted at booking, with no surprises.

Self-Pickup

Prefer to come to us? Self-pickup is available at our location. The address is shared with your confirmation.

Anywhere in New England

Need the car somewhere we haven't listed? Delivery elsewhere in New England is available on request.
