The Fine Print

Questions

Is membership really free?

Yes. Every tier is earned by booking with us, never bought. Upper Echelon is by invitation only.

When do I get my number?

As soon as your first booking is complete, whether it's a car, a charter, a flight or an evening. It's yours for life and never changes.

How do I reach Black?

Three completed bookings, or $10,000 with us, within any 12 months. Or book on the road, on the water and in the air within a year. You keep your tier through the following year.

What counts toward my tier?

Every Echelon Exotics, Chauffeur, Boat, Jet and Experiences booking. Jet Card holders are invited to Upper Echelon.

Can I rent an exotic at 21?

Membership starts at 21, but each service keeps its own requirements. For example, exotic and luxury car drivers must be 25 or older.

Can I share my membership?

Memberships are personal and non-transferable. Perks depend on availability and have no cash value. See the Membership Terms.
