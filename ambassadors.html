FAQ

Ambassador Questions

Who can apply?

Anyone 21 or older. You don't need a big following. Plenty of our best referrals come from people who simply know others who celebrate in style.

How much do I earn?

10% of the rental price on every booking made through your link. Your dashboard shows every booking and what you've earned.

When do I get paid?

Commission is earned once a referred rental is completed. We pay out your balance using the payout method you choose, such as Zelle.

How do referrals get tracked?

When someone opens your link, it's saved on their device for 60 days. If they book an exotic rental in that time, the booking is credited to you.

Can I use my own link to book?

Bookings you make for yourself don't earn commission. The program is for the people you bring to Echelon.

Does it cost anything?

No. The program is free to join, and there are no minimums or quotas.
